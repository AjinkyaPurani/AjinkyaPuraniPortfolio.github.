About Me

I'm a Supply Chain professional with 3+ years of experience in replenishment, inventory optimization, logistics, analytics, and warehouse operations, delivering $2.2M+ in cost savings through process improvement and data-driven decision-making.

I progressed from Amazon Warehouse Associate to Warehouse Lead, where I led 50+ associates and built Power BI dashboards to improve operational visibility, KPI tracking, and productivity performance.

I expanded my supply chain expertise in retail and academic environments, improving inventory accuracy to 92% at Michaels while supporting logistics and demand planning education as a Graduate Teaching Assistant.

At Canadian Tire Corporation, I managed large-scale replenishment and inventory optimization across 6,000+ SKUs and 500+ stores, partnering with 150+ vendors to improve availability, reduce stockouts, and strengthen inventory efficiency across the network.

Currently, as a Replenishment Support Analyst II, I focus on replenishment analytics, reporting automation, process improvement, and enabling merchandising decisions through data insights. I leverage Advanced Excel, SQL, Python, R Programming, and Power BI to streamline reporting and drive operational efficiency.

I am focused on building scalable, efficient, and resilient supply chain systems powered by analytics and continuous improvement.
